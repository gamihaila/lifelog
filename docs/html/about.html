About the Author

George Mihaila was born in Romania and trained as a mathematician, with a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Toronto. He emigrated to Canada in 1993, moved to New York State in 2000, and finally settled in California in 2012. He has worked at IBM Research, Columbia University, Google, and Megagon Labs, and he is now at Apple. He lives in Mountain View, California, with his wife, Grațiela; they have four daughters.
