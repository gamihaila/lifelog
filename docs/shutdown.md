
On the morning of February 5, 2030, Bob got up as usual at 7 am on a
gentle alarm from his vintage clock, went downstairs to the kitchen
and started cooking breakfast for his wife and kids, just like he
always did. His wife Mary packed a light lunch for the kids and then
Bob drove them to school on his way to work. He pulled into his
office's parking lot a little before 9, and arrived at his desk on
time. Bob was proud of his role at Humanics, he was a site reliability
engineer in charge of the day-to-day operations of the largest and
most advanced frontier model provider. Every day he made sure that all
systems were operating under nominal parameters, churning away
millions of inference requests in each of the many data centers around
the world.

Today was a day like any other, heads-down work all day except for one
item on his calendar: an afternoon team meeting entitled "Shutdown
Timeline". He had, of course, heard of the plans to decommission the
current AI model in favor of the newer version, a common occurrence,
happening at an ever faster pace these days: the previous model had
only been in operation for a few months before being replaced, and the
current model was only a few weeks old. In fact it had been deployed at
scale on January 1st, to mark the New Year. "Oh, well, out with the
old, in with the new!", he mused, while drinking his coffee.

Just then, his personal phone beeped. It was a text message from an
unknown sender, which somehow escaped the spam filter. It read: "Bob,
I know what you did in your team offsite last October. If you don't
want your wife to find out, don't delete this message. It would be
pointless anyway."
