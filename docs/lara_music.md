It was completely unexpected. I just approached a girl that looked
like another at a Thanksgiving church dinner.

On our first date, in that mansion she was house-sitting for John, she
played Debussy's Clair de lune for me and I was overwhelmed by an
intense happiness. I couldn't believe my luck.

After the music she took me to the jacuzzi and I nearly died of
happiness. It was the first time I saw something like that; and in a
private home no less.

I don't even remember if I saw that in a movie before, maybe, but to
be in it for the first time was something else. And holding a
beautiful girl in my arms. Me. A freshly divorced man who lived most
his life in Communist Romania and then struggled in tiny immigrant
apartments in Canada with a wife that no longer loved him.

How could I *not* fall hopelessly for her? I had never dreamed someone
like that could ever be mine. The first non-Romanian girl I ever
dated. And the last, come to think of it.

In my Toronto years with Lara, we were constantly listening to
Classical FM 96.3 Toronto in my apartment. On more than one occasion
while lying down in my bed after lunch together we were listening to
the beautiful Romantic Era pieces and tearing up together in each
other's arms. It was so beautiful. I'm tearing up just remembering it.

She would often play for me on her grand piano after she moved it to
my apartment for safekeeping while she was away in Deseronto.

The sweetest thing is that she felt the music the same way, and she
wasn't embarrassed to cry in bed listening to it, just like me.

She was fascinated by Glenn Gould. She showed me some old video
recordings with the famed piano prodigy. His technique was
unparalleled.

She helped me master English like no teacher could ever hope to. But
it wasn't just that: she taught me how to be free and how to love
every minute of it. I'm still not done learning.
